HEALTH, SAFETY AND ENVIRONMENT (HSE) POLICY

Revision: 7.2

Owner: HSE Manager (Henry Nakamura)
Classification: Public

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This policy establishes the HSE expectations for all NorthStar Petroleum Corporation
operations including upstream drilling and production, midstream pipelines, downstream
refining, and corporate functions. It applies to every employee, contractor, vendor, and
visitor on company controlled premises and to any person acting on behalf of the company.

2. POLICY STATEMENT
NorthStar Petroleum is committed to operating in a manner that protects the health and
safety of people, safeguards the environment, and respects the communities in which we
operate. We pursue an injury-free, illness-free, and incident-free workplace as a non-
negotiable business objective.

3. CORE PRINCIPLES
3.1 Stop Work Authority. Every person has the unconditional right and duty to stop any
    activity considered unsafe. No retribution shall result from invoking stop-work.
3.2 Hierarchy of Controls. Hazards shall be addressed through elimination, substitution,
    engineering controls, administrative controls, and personal protective equipment in
    that order of preference.
3.3 Process Safety. We apply API RP 754 leading and lagging indicators to pipelines,
    refineries, and high consequence wells. Tier 1 and Tier 2 events trigger formal
    incident investigation under the Incident Response Plan (NSP-HSE-PLN-005).
3.4 Environmental Stewardship. Spills, fugitive emissions, and exceedances of permitted
    limits are treated as serious events regardless of regulatory threshold.

4. RESPONSIBILITIES
4.1 Chief Executive Officer: ultimate accountability; approves the annual HSE plan.
4.2 Chief Operating Officer: ensures HSE is integrated into all operations decisions.
4.3 HSE Manager: maintains the management system, leads serious incident investigations,
    reports to the Board HSE Committee quarterly.
4.4 Field Supervisors: conduct daily safety briefings, verify permit-to-work, ensure
    contractors comply with this policy.
4.5 Every Employee: report all near misses, first aid, and unsafe acts within 24 hours
    via the incident reporting system.

5. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS
The following KPIs are reported monthly and reviewed by the Board HSE Committee:
- Total Recordable Incident Rate (TRIR), target less than 0.20 per 200,000 hours
- Lost Time Injury Frequency (LTIF), target less than 0.05
- Process Safety Event Rate Tier 1, target zero
- Reportable spills > 1 barrel, target zero
- Methane emissions intensity, target less than 0.20 percent of production

6. NON COMPLIANCE
Wilful disregard of this policy is grounds for disciplinary action up to termination.
Contractor companies that breach the policy may have their pre-qualification revoked
and outstanding purchase orders cancelled.
