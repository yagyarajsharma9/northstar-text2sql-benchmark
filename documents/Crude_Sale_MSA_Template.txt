CRUDE OIL SALE AGREEMENT - MASTER TEMPLATE
Form: NSP-LEG-TPL-CRUDE-MSA-2026
Owner: Legal (Kira Takahashi)

This Master Sale Agreement ("MSA") is entered into between NorthStar Petroleum
Corporation ("Seller") and the Buyer named on the cover page. Specific deliveries
are governed by individual confirmations referencing this MSA.

ARTICLE 1 PRODUCT AND QUALITY
1.1 The Crude Oil sold under this MSA is identified by NorthStar product code WTI,
    BRENT, or MARS as applicable. Quality specifications are those published in the
    most recent crude_assays record for the originating field.
1.2 If at the point of delivery the API gravity deviates by more than 1.0 degrees from
    the published assay, the Buyer may either accept with a price adjustment per the
    standard differential schedule or reject the parcel.
1.3 Sulfur content above contractual maximum gives Buyer the right to a discount per
    the differential schedule, or to reject if above 110 percent of maximum.

ARTICLE 2 QUANTITY AND TOLERANCE
Each confirmation states a nominal volume in barrels and a tolerance, typically plus or
minus 5 percent at Seller's option. Loaded volume is the basis of invoice unless the
discharge survey is more than 0.30 percent different, in which case the parties agree
to the average of load and discharge.

ARTICLE 3 DELIVERY AND TITLE
Delivery is FOB load port unless the confirmation specifies otherwise. Title and risk
pass at the flange connection between the loading arm and the vessel manifold.

ARTICLE 4 PRICE
Unless stated otherwise the price is the average of NYMEX WTI front month settlements
for the five business days starting on the bill of lading date, plus or minus the
applicable differential.

ARTICLE 5 PAYMENT
Payment is by telegraphic transfer in US Dollars to the bank account designated by
Seller, on the basis of NET 30 days from bill of lading, against presentation of
commercial invoice and copy of the bill of lading. Late payment accrues interest at
SOFR plus 2 percent per annum.

ARTICLE 6 DEMURRAGE AND LAYTIME
Laytime is 36 hours allowed plus 6 hours notice of readiness, unless otherwise stated.
Demurrage rate is the lower of the charter party rate and the AFRA rate for the
relevant vessel size class.

ARTICLE 7 FORCE MAJEURE
Neither party is liable for failure or delay arising from acts of God, war, insurrection,
strike, governmental action, severe weather, or other event beyond reasonable control.
Notice must be given within 48 hours.

ARTICLE 8 SANCTIONS AND COMPLIANCE
Buyer warrants that it is not, and is not owned by, any person on the OFAC SDN list,
the EU consolidated sanctions list, or the UK HMT financial sanctions list. Buyer will
provide load port and discharge port and the vessel's IMO and ownership chain prior to
nomination. Seller may refuse a nomination that fails compliance screening.

ARTICLE 9 ENVIRONMENTAL
Buyer shall not co-mingle the cargo with any product known to be sourced from
sanctioned origin. Buyer shall ensure the receiving terminal complies with vapor
recovery and ballast water regulations applicable at the discharge port.

ARTICLE 10 GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES
Texas law governs (excluding conflicts of laws). Disputes are settled by arbitration
under the rules of the ICC, seat Houston, in English, by three arbitrators.

ARTICLE 11 NOTICES
All notices to NorthStar shall be addressed to the General Counsel, with a copy to the
relationship manager named on the cover page.

ARTICLE 12 ENTIRE AGREEMENT
This MSA, together with each confirmation, is the entire agreement between the parties
on the subject matter, superseding prior negotiations and prior agreements.
