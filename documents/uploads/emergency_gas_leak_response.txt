NORTHSTAR PETROLEUM CORPORATION
EMERGENCY GAS LEAK RESPONSE PROCEDURE
Document ID: NSP-HSE-PRC-099
Revision: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-04-15
Owner: HSE Manager

PURPOSE
Defines immediate response steps for any unexpected gas leak detected at a NorthStar
facility, including pipelines, well sites, refineries, and storage terminals.

CLASSIFICATION
Category Alpha: detected concentration of methane below 1000 ppm OR H2S below 10 ppm
Category Bravo: methane between 1000 and 10000 ppm OR H2S between 10 and 50 ppm
Category Charlie: methane above 10000 ppm OR H2S above 50 ppm OR any visible flame
Category Delta: large uncontained release with imminent risk to life

IMMEDIATE STEPS
1. Stop work in the affected zone and surrounding 100 meters.
2. Evacuate non-essential personnel upwind to the nearest muster point.
3. Notify the Incident Commander on duty within 5 minutes.
4. Isolate the source via remote shut-in valves where available.
5. Do not attempt to ignite or extinguish flames without IC approval.

PROCEDURAL TIMELINE
T+0:    Detection by sensor or observation. Trip the local alarm.
T+5:    Operations Manager notified. Ventilation systems set to maximum exhaust.
T+10:   HSE Officer on scene with portable detection equipment.
T+15:   Incident logged in incidents table with severity Bravo or higher.
T+30:   First update to COO if Charlie or Delta. Comms team standby.
T+60:   Root cause working theory documented. External notification prepared.

POST-EVENT
Within 24 hours: investigation team assembled with reporter, validator, approver chain.
Within 30 days: corrective actions tracked as work_orders linked to the incident_id.
Within 90 days: lessons learned shared cross-asset and added to the next safety audit.

KPI TARGETS
Mean detection-to-isolation time: less than 5 minutes for Category Charlie.
Recurrence rate at the same asset: zero within 12 months after corrective action.
