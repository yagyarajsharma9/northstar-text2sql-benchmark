NORTHSTAR PETROLEUM CORPORATION
VENDOR ONBOARDING CHECKLIST
Document ID: NSP-PRC-CHK-002
Revision: 6.0
Effective Date: 2026-02-15
Owner: Procurement (Ava Singh)

PHASE 1 PREQUALIFICATION
[ ] Complete vendor registration form, capture in vendors table
[ ] Tax ID and W-8 or W-9 verified
[ ] Banking details verified by phone callback to a known number
[ ] Watch list screening: OFAC, EU sanctions, UK sanctions, Canadian sanctions
[ ] Politically exposed persons screening, refresh every 12 months
[ ] Modern Slavery Act statement reviewed for vendors above 36 million USD turnover
[ ] Beneficial ownership disclosure for entities outside FATF aligned jurisdictions

PHASE 2 HSE QUALIFICATION
[ ] OSHA TRIR for last three years, target less than 1.5
[ ] EMR (workers compensation experience modifier), target less than 1.0
[ ] ISO 45001 or equivalent occupational safety management system
[ ] ISO 14001 or equivalent environmental management system
[ ] Site visit by HSE officer if vendor will perform work on company premises
[ ] Methane emissions disclosure for upstream service providers per OGCI guidance

PHASE 3 TECHNICAL QUALIFICATION
[ ] Reference checks, minimum two operator references in last 24 months
[ ] Equipment list with maintenance and inspection records
[ ] Personnel competency records: well control, H2S, confined space, working at height
[ ] Insurance certificates: CGL 5 million USD per occurrence, professional liability
    where applicable, environmental impairment for high risk activity
[ ] Cyber security questionnaire NSP-IT-FRM-009 for IT vendors

PHASE 4 COMMERCIAL
[ ] Master Services Agreement signed and stored in document_references
[ ] Pricing schedule and rate card validated against benchmark
[ ] Payment terms aligned with vendor master, default Net 45
[ ] Currency confirmation, default USD with FX hedging beyond 90 day exposure
[ ] Contract record created in contracts table with status Draft

PHASE 5 SYSTEM SETUP
[ ] Vendor record created in SAP S/4HANA, link saved in external_links
[ ] Vendor portal account provisioned (e.g. Coupa)
[ ] Spend categorization in cost_centers
[ ] Procurement Manager approval recorded via workflow WF-PO-LOW or WF-PO-HIGH

PHASE 6 ACTIVATION
[ ] First purchase order limited to 250,000 USD until two completed deliveries
[ ] First invoice cycle reviewed by AP team and noted in audit_log
[ ] Performance review scheduled at 90 days and 12 months

OWNERSHIP
Procurement Manager owns this checklist end to end. Items 1, 2, 4 are mandatory
controls and may not be waived. Items 3 and 5 may be waived only by the Chief
Operating Officer with documented justification.
