NORTHSTAR PETROLEUM CORPORATION
PRODUCTION DATA VALIDATION PROCEDURE
Document ID: NSP-PRO-PRC-021
Revision: 3.4
Effective Date: 2025-12-01
Owner: Production Engineering (Aria Kowalski)

1. PURPOSE
Sets the standard for capturing, validating, and locking daily production volumes for
oil, gas, and water across all NorthStar operated wells. Validated production figures
are the basis for royalty payments, reserves reporting, and shareholder disclosure, so
data integrity is paramount.

2. ROLES
- Reporter: Field Supervisor or Production Engineer at the asset
- Validator: Production Engineer designated as VALIDATOR role for the field
- Approver:  Operations Manager for that field

The reporter, validator, and approver MUST be three different individuals.

3. DAILY CYCLE
3.1 By 06:00 local, the field operator captures previous day volumes from automation
    (SCADA) or from manual gauge in the daily_production table.
3.2 By 09:00 local, the field supervisor reviews and submits the entries.
3.3 By 12:00 local, the assigned validator runs the validation checks (Section 4).
3.4 By 17:00 local, the operations manager approves the validated volumes for the
    field. Once approved, the records are locked.

4. VALIDATION CHECKS
The validator confirms each daily record passes:
- Mass balance: oil_bbl + water_bbl matches metered liquid within 2 percent
- GOR check: gas_mscf divided by oil_bbl within plus or minus 25 percent of the
  3-day rolling average
- Runtime: runtime_hours less than or equal to 24
- Choke and pressure consistency with historical operating envelope
- Reservoir model alignment: no greater than 30 percent deviation from the latest
  forecast volume curve maintained by Reservoir Engineering

If any check fails, the record is rejected back to the reporter with a comment in the
notifications table; the reporter has 24 hours to amend or to escalate.

5. EXCEPTIONS AND DOWNTIME
For wells with status ShutIn or Suspended, only the runtime hours and pressures are
required; volumes default to zero. Routine maintenance shutdowns are recorded with the
work_orders id in the notes field. Unplanned downtime greater than 24 hours triggers
an incident review.

6. MONTH-END LOCK
On the third business day of the following month, all daily records for the prior month
are batch-locked by the system. After lock, changes require an approval workflow
identical to a Tier 1 AFE.

7. SCADA TIE-IN
The OSIsoft PI historian is the system of record for raw automation data. The reference
in external_links provides the audit trail. Discrepancies between SCADA and the
manual entry greater than 5 percent are flagged for the validator.

8. KPI
Validation completion rate by 12:00 target: 95 percent of producing wells.
Average validator turnaround target: less than 3 hours.
