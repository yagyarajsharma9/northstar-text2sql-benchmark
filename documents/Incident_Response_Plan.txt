NORTHSTAR PETROLEUM CORPORATION
INCIDENT RESPONSE PLAN
Document ID: NSP-HSE-PLN-005
Revision: 8.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-15
Owner: HSE Manager (Henry Nakamura)

1. PURPOSE
This plan defines NorthStar's response to operational incidents including injuries,
spills, fires, gas releases, and equipment failures. It assigns responsibility and
sets expectations for notification, investigation, root cause analysis, and corrective
actions.

2. INCIDENT CLASSIFICATION
Severity follows the corporate Significant Injury or Fatality (SIF) framework:
- SIF1 (Negligible): no injury, no environmental impact, less than 10,000 USD cost
- SIF2 (Minor):      first aid, minor spill contained, 10,000 to 100,000 USD
- SIF3 (Moderate):   medical treatment, restricted work, 100,000 to 1,000,000 USD
- SIF4 (Major):      lost time injury, reportable spill, 1,000,000 to 10,000,000 USD
- SIF5 (Catastrophic): fatality, major release, above 10,000,000 USD

3. NOTIFICATION TIMELINE
Within 1 hour:
- SIF4 and SIF5 to HSE Manager and COO via Push notification
Within 4 hours:
- SIF3 to HSE Manager and operations management
Within 24 hours:
- All incidents recorded in the incidents table with reported_by_user_id
- Notification sent to investigated_by_user_id

External notifications shall meet the more stringent of corporate or regulatory
requirements; the Compliance team holds the master notification matrix.

4. INVESTIGATION
Every SIF3 and above triggers a formal investigation:
- Lead investigator assigned within 24 hours
- TapRoot or 5-Why method used as default; complex cases escalated to ICAM
- Site preserved until investigators release the scene
- Final report within 30 days (SIF3) or 60 days (SIF4 and SIF5)
- root_cause and corrective_actions fields populated and signed off by HSE Manager

5. CORRECTIVE ACTIONS
Each corrective action is tracked as a work_orders record with priority based on
severity. Action owners report status weekly until closure. Internal Audit verifies
closure for SIF3 and above.

6. ENVIRONMENTAL EVENTS
Spills:
- Less than 1 bbl: clean up, record in incidents
- 1 to 50 bbl: notify HSE Manager and regulator if reportable threshold met
- Greater than 50 bbl: activate Emergency Response Team, evacuate as needed
Releases:
- H2S above 100 ppm at perimeter: shut in source, evacuate downwind
- Methane release above 100 kg or 30 minutes: notify regulator, deploy IR camera

7. COMMUNICATIONS
The Communications Team is the single point of contact for media. No employee shall
communicate with media without written authorization. Internal communications use the
notifications table for affected and adjacent personnel.

8. REGULATORY REPORTING
Reportable events are filed by Compliance within mandated timeframes. The
regulatory_filings table records the filing reference and status. External regulator
links are stored in external_links with system_name set to the agency code.

9. RECORDS RETENTION
Incident records retained for 30 years. Investigation reports for the life of the
asset plus 10 years. SIF4 and SIF5 documentation retained permanently.

10. DRILLS
Quarterly tabletop drills at every operating site. Annual full-scale drill including
regulators and mutual aid partners. Drill outcomes stored as inspection records of
type Routine PM with finding_summary describing performance.
