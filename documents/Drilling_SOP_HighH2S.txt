NORTHSTAR PETROLEUM CORPORATION
STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
DRILLING IN HIGH H2S ENVIRONMENTS
Document ID: NSP-DRL-SOP-018
Revision: 4.1
Effective Date: 2025-09-15
Owner: Drilling Engineering (Amir Haddad)

1. SCOPE
This SOP applies to drilling, completion, and workover operations on wells where the
forecast hydrogen sulfide (H2S) concentration in produced fluids may exceed 20 parts per
million (ppm). It supplements the corporate HSE Policy NSP-HSE-POL-001.

2. CLASSIFICATION
Wells are classified by the highest expected H2S in mol percent of the gas phase:
- Sweet:        less than 4 ppm     standard procedures
- Sour Class 1: 4 to 100 ppm        SOP 018 light controls
- Sour Class 2: 100 to 1000 ppm     SOP 018 full controls
- Sour Class 3: above 1000 ppm      SOP 018 full controls plus written deviation

3. PRE-SPUD REQUIREMENTS
3.1 Hazard analysis: a HAZID and a quantitative risk assessment must be reviewed by
    the Drilling Engineer of record and signed off by the Operations Manager.
3.2 Materials: all wellhead, BOP, and downhole tubulars in contact with sour fluids must
    meet NACE MR0175 requirements. Documentation retained for 25 years.
3.3 Detection: a minimum of four fixed H2S monitors and one wireless personal monitor per
    crew member. Alarm set points: low 5 ppm, high 10 ppm, very high 20 ppm.
3.4 Egress: two upwind muster points, escape breathing apparatus rated 30 minutes,
    weekly emergency drills.

4. OPERATIONAL CONTROLS
4.1 Drilling fluid pH must be maintained above 10.5 using lime; sulfide scavenger
    (zinc carbonate) carried at 2 to 5 lb per barrel.
4.2 Trip tank monitored continuously; any unexplained gain over 0.5 bbl triggers a flow
    check and possible shut-in.
4.3 Flares lit and pilots verified prior to circulating gas to surface.
4.4 Personnel without current H2S training (per OSHA 29 CFR 1910.120) shall not be on
    the rig floor during sour intervals.

5. DAILY PRODUCTION DATA HANDLING
On wells in sour service, the daily production form must record H2S concentration in the
casinghead gas at minimum once per shift. Values exceeding 100 ppm above the previous
24-hour rolling average shall be flagged in the production system and validated by the
Production Engineer before management reporting.

6. INCIDENT RESPONSE
Any H2S release exceeding 50 ppm at the perimeter, or any personnel exposure above
10 ppm time-weighted, is classified at minimum SIF3 and escalated immediately to the
HSE Manager. The well shall be shut in pending investigation.

7. APPROVAL CHAIN FOR DEVIATIONS
Any deviation from this SOP requires the approval workflow WF-WELL-AFE plus an
additional sign-off by the HSE Manager. Deviations are recorded in the SCADA system
with cross reference to the corresponding approval_request record.

8. RECORDS
Daily drilling reports, mud reports, BOP test certificates, and H2S monitor calibration
logs are retained for the life of the well plus 10 years in SharePoint and indexed in
the document_references table.
