NORTHSTAR PETROLEUM CORPORATION
PIPELINE INTEGRITY MANAGEMENT PROGRAM
Document ID: NSP-MID-PRG-011
Revision: 3.0
Effective Date: 2026-03-01
Owner: Midstream Operations (Ethan Zhao)

1. SCOPE
This program covers all crude, refined product, gas, and NGL pipelines operated by the
NorthStar Midstream business unit. It complies with 49 CFR 192 and 195 (US), CSA Z662
(Canada), and applicable host country regulations.

2. RISK CATEGORIZATION
Pipelines are categorized into High Consequence Areas (HCA), Moderate, and Low based on
proximity to populated areas, drinking water sources, ecologically sensitive areas, and
commercially navigable waterways. Categorization is reviewed every two years or after
any change in surrounding land use.

3. ASSESSMENT METHODS
3.1 In-Line Inspection (ILI). Magnetic flux leakage (MFL) and ultrasonic tools deployed
    on a 5 year cycle for HCA, 7 year for Moderate, 10 year for Low. Results are stored
    in the inspections table with finding_severity assigned per Section 5.
3.2 Direct Assessment. ECDA, ICDA, or SCCDA employed where ILI is not feasible.
3.3 Hydrostatic Pressure Test. Required after major repair or replacement of segments
    rated for pressure greater than 60 percent of specified minimum yield strength.

4. THRESHOLD VALUES
A pipeline_segments record with metal loss exceeding 40 percent of nominal wall thickness
is automatically routed to the Maintenance Repair queue. Loss exceeding 60 percent is
classified as a critical finding requiring an immediate pressure reduction and an
emergency work_order with priority Critical.

5. SEVERITY MAPPING
- None:    No anomaly or anomaly less than 10 percent metal loss
- Low:     10 to 25 percent
- Medium:  25 to 40 percent
- High:    40 to 60 percent
- Critical: above 60 percent or any leak

6. RESPONSE TIMELINE
- Critical: act within 24 hours, segment isolation if leak
- High:    act within 30 days
- Medium:  schedule within 12 months
- Low:     monitor at next planned inspection

7. RECORDS AND DATA FLOW
- Inspection report (PDF) loaded to S3, document_references record created
- Findings keyed into inspections table and linked via report_external_link_id
- Repair work_order linked back to pipeline_segments by segment_id
- KPIs feed the Quarterly Asset Integrity Review

8. EXTERNAL REPORTING
Reportable conditions are filed with the regulator within the timeframes mandated by the
applicable rule, generally 30 days for safety related conditions in HCA. The Compliance
team maintains the regulatory_filings ledger.

9. ROLES
Pipeline Supervisor (Sara Popov, Tomas Silva): execute field assessments
Midstream Manager (Ethan Zhao): approves the annual integrity plan
HSE Manager (Henry Nakamura): independent oversight of critical findings
Internal Audit: annual program audit

10. KPI
- Percent of HCA mileage assessed on schedule: target 100 percent
- Critical findings closed within mandate: target 100 percent
- Mean time to repair, High severity: target less than 30 days
