NORTHSTAR PETROLEUM CORPORATION
QUARTERLY PRODUCTION REPORT - Q4 2025

Issued: 2026-01-22
Owner: Production Engineering (Aria Kowalski)
Distribution: Executive Team, Board, Reservoir Engineering, Investor Relations

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
NorthStar produced an aggregate average of 187,400 barrels of oil equivalent per day
(boe/d) during Q4 2025, a 3.1 percent increase quarter-over-quarter and 8.6 percent
year-over-year. Liquids accounted for 71 percent of the total. Production beat the
internal plan of 182,000 boe/d driven by stronger Permian Basin performance and an
earlier-than-expected restart of two North Sea wells.

KEY METRICS
Production by stream
- Crude oil:       109,200 bbl/d   (plan 105,000)
- NGL:              23,800 bbl/d   (plan 22,500)
- Natural gas:     327 MMcf/d      (plan 318)

Production by field (oil bbl/d, average)
- Eagle Ford Permian (PERM-EAG): 56,200
- Bakken Williston (BAK-WIL):    18,400
- Mars Deepwater (GOM-MAR):      14,900
- Brent North Sea (NS-BRENT):     8,600
- Ghawar South (MID-GHAR):        9,300 (working interest share)
- Athabasca (CAN-ATH):            1,800

WELL ACTIVITY
- 7 new producers brought online (5 Permian, 2 Bakken)
- 12 wells underwent workover, 3 remain shut-in pending parts
- Average completion cycle time: 41 days, down from 47 days in Q3
- AFE accuracy: 91.4 percent (target 90 percent)

KEY HSE NOTES
- TRIR Q4 2025: 0.18 (target less than 0.20)
- One Tier 2 process safety event (separator overpressure at Eagle Ford), root cause
  identified, no environmental impact, no injury
- Two reportable spills, both contained within secondary containment
- Methane intensity: 0.17 percent, on track for 2026 target

DECLINE ANALYSIS
The portfolio-level base decline was measured at 19 percent on an annualized basis,
slightly improved from the 21 percent guided in the 2025 plan. The improvement is
attributable to optimized choke programs at Eagle Ford and successful gas lift
installations at three Bakken wells.

FINANCIAL CONSIDERATIONS
- Average realized crude price: 78.40 USD per bbl
- Lifting cost: 8.95 USD per bbl, slightly above plan due to weather-related downtime
- Royalty payments: 41.2 million USD
- 14 contracts active for crude sales; one master agreement renewed with Phillips 66
  for 75,000 bbl/d through 2027

DATA QUALITY
99.6 percent of daily_production records were validated within the procedural SLA. The
remaining 0.4 percent (mostly Athabasca steam-assisted gravity drainage cells) were
held due to flow meter calibration drift identified by the validator team.

OUTLOOK Q1 2026
- Plan: 191,500 boe/d, 2.2 percent higher quarter-on-quarter
- Risks: pipeline outage on PL-CRUDE-02 scheduled for maintenance week of 2026-02-15;
  potential weather impact in North Dakota
- Mitigants: temporary trucking arrangement secured with Vendor VEND-0037
