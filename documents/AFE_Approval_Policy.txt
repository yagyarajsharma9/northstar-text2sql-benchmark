NORTHSTAR PETROLEUM CORPORATION
AUTHORIZATION FOR EXPENDITURE (AFE) APPROVAL POLICY
Document ID: NSP-FIN-POL-007
Revision: 5.0
Effective Date: 2026-04-01
Owner: Chief Financial Officer (David Chen)

1. PURPOSE
Defines authority levels and the chain of approval for capital and major operating
expenditures. Aligns delegations and approval workflows with Article VII of the
corporate by-laws and the latest Internal Audit charter.

2. AFE THRESHOLDS AND CHAIN
Tier 1: less than 100,000 USD
   - Originator: Engineer or Supervisor
   - Validator:  Cost Center Owner
   - Approver:   Department Manager
   - Workflow:   WF-PO-LOW

Tier 2: 100,000 to 1,000,000 USD
   - Originator: Engineer
   - Validator:  Department Manager
   - Approver:   COO or CFO (whichever is closer to scope)
   - Workflow:   WF-PO-HIGH or WF-WELL-AFE

Tier 3: 1,000,000 to 10,000,000 USD
   - Originator: Department Manager
   - Validator:  COO
   - Approver:   CFO and CEO (joint)
   - Workflow:   WF-WELL-AFE

Tier 4: above 10,000,000 USD
   - Same as Tier 3 plus Board of Directors approval, recorded by minute resolution.

3. APPROVAL PRINCIPLES
3.1 No self-approval. The originator may not approve their own AFE at any tier.
3.2 Independence. Validators must not report into the approver in the same line.
3.3 Delegation. CEO, CFO, and COO may delegate authority through the delegations
    table for up to 30 calendar days. Delegations must be active at the moment of
    approval; back-dated approvals are invalid.

4. SUPPORTING DOCUMENTATION
Every AFE approval request shall include:
- Scope statement and business justification
- Cost breakdown linked to cost_centers
- Supplier prequalification or competitive bid summary
- Risk assessment, including HSE and reputational items
- Reference to any related contract or master agreement

5. RECORDING IN SYSTEMS
The approval record is created in the approval_requests table and each approval action
in approval_actions. The corresponding purchase_order or drilling_operations record
must reference approval_request_id. Documents are stored as document_references with
a SharePoint or S3 storage_uri.

6. AUDIT REQUIREMENTS
Internal Audit shall sample at least 5 percent of AFEs each quarter and verify:
- Original requestor has not also approved
- All required steps in approval_steps were executed
- Supporting documents are linked
- Variance from AFE to actual cost in drilling_operations is within 10 percent
  for Tier 1, 15 percent for Tier 2, and 20 percent for Tier 3 and above

7. NON COMPLIANCE
AFE bypasses are reportable to the Audit Committee. Repeat or material bypasses are
disciplinary events under the Code of Business Conduct.
